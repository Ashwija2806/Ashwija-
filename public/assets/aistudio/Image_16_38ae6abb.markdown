## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-energy, trend-conscious, and conversion-optimized retail experience. The visual language blends the punchy energy of modern fashion publishing with the friction-free utility of algorithmic shopping.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Impulsive & Euphoric:** Visual cues inspire discovery and excitement through saturated accents and clear micro-affirmations.
- **Authoritative & Curated:** Crisp typographic hierarchy, precise baseline rhythms, and refined contrast give editorial legitimacy to high-volume inventory.
- **Effortless & Immediate:** Interface boundaries recede to push merchandise forward, prioritizing instant scannability and clear calls to action.

### Design Movement: Modern High-Street E-Commerce
The visual grammar leans into modern fashion utility: high-contrast surfaces, razor-sharp organizational geometry, compact rating badges, and vibrant accent triggers. Clean micro-elevations deliver tactile response without visual weight, keeping the focus entirely on lifestyle imagery and quick checkout pathways.

## Colors

The palette leverages a high-saturation primary signature complemented by functional retail indicators. Color guides users toward conversions, discounts, and inventory signals.

### Color Roles & Application
- **Primary (`#FF3F6C`):** Reserved for high-intent moments: primary purchase buttons, bag badges, selected filter toggles, active navigation indicators, and primary wishlisting states.
- **Secondary (`#FF905A`):** Applied to secondary badges, flash-deal countdown indicators, promotional banners, and urgent merchandising accents.
- **Tertiary / Success (`#03A685`):** Denotes percentage discounts, stock availability cues ("In Stock"), rating chips, and successful transaction states.
- **Dark Text / Heading (`#282C3F`):** Deep charcoal used for brand headers, key titles, numeric values, and dark icon fills. Delivers stronger legibility than pure black.
- **Muted Text (`#7E818C`):** Used for auxiliary product categorization, crossed-out original prices (MRP), search placeholders, and inactive tab labels.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#EAEAEC`):** Structural borders, card boundaries, horizontal separators, and form field resting states.
- **Backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` & `#F5F5F6`):** Pure white serves as the primary canvas for maximum product contrast. Cool off-white (`#F5F5F6`) sets apart secondary carousels, filter sidebars, promo banners, and app navigation bars.
- **Highlight Accent (`#FF5722`):** Reserved strictly for scarcity alerts, limited-time price drops, and clearance stickers.

## Typography

The type system is built with **Inter** for fast visual scanning, structural density, and clarity across low and high DPI screens.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Styling
- **Brand Titles (`title-brand`):** Rendered in uppercase with slight tracking (`0.06em`) to establish visual anchor points in product feeds.
- **Product Titles (`title-product`):** Standard sentence case with a neutral weight to keep product descriptions secondary to brand and price.
- **Pricing Cluster:** The core commercial anchor. The current selling price is bold and dark (`#282C3F`), immediately paired with the strikethrough original price in muted grey (`#7E818C`), and finished with the discount tag in emerald (`#03A685`) or secondary orange (`#FF905A`).
- **Navigation Items (`label-nav`):** Bold uppercase labels provide structured, category-level browsing clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8pt spacing grid establishes rhythm, scale, and density across devices.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid centered inside a max-width container of `1440px`. Column gutters remain `1.5rem` (`24px`) with safe outer margins of `2.5rem` (`40px`). E-commerce catalog pages allocate 3 columns (`25%`) to filters and 9 columns (`75%`) to merchandise grids (displaying 4 products per row).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters. Product listings shift to 3 columns per row with collapsible filter headers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 2-column fluid layout with an exact `0.5rem` (`8px`) or `0.75rem` (`12px`) inner gutter to maximize image real estate. Outer canvas margins default to `1rem` (`16px`).

### Reflow Rules
- Headers collapse into a top search-and-action row on mobile, while primary category navigation moves to an edge-to-edge horizontal snap carousel.
- Filter sidebars convert to a floating, fixed bottom-action bar ("Sort / Filter") triggering bottom sheet viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Surfaces rely on clean surface layering, thin border definition, and crisp ambient drops to keep product imagery front and center.

### Elevation Layers
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Used for base page canvas and nested modules. Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or cool off-white (`#F5F5F6`) with no drop shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card Resting & Inline UI):** Product cards, input fields, and category tags rely primarily on a `1px` structural outline (`#EAEAEC`). On mobile, cards sit borderless over `#F5F5F6` or bordered over `#FFFFFF`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns):** Interactive cards elevate on desktop hover using an ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(40, 44, 63, 0.08)`. Navigation mega-menus and search suggestion boxes apply `0 8px 24px rgba(40, 44, 63, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Chrome & Floating Drawers):** The sticky header applies an ultra-subtle under-border (`1px solid #EAEAEC`) plus `0 2px 8px rgba(40, 44, 63, 0.04)`. Mobile bottom sheets and sticky checkout footers use `0 -4px 20px rgba(40, 44, 63, 0.1)`.
- **Level 4 (Modal & Overlays):** Full checkout dialogues and size guides use `0 16px 36px rgba(40, 44, 63, 0.2)` backed by a `rgba(40, 44, 63, 0.6)` backdrop scrim.

## Shapes

The interface uses compact rounded corners to preserve a sharp, contemporary retail look without drifting into playful or overly casual territory.

### Corner Radius Usage
- **Micro Radius (`2px - 4px`):** Used on product card images, rating badges, discount chips, text inputs, and secondary badges. Keeps lines crisp and structural.
- **Base Radius (`4px - 6px`):** Used on primary buttons, filter tags, dropdown menus, and cart notification snackbars.
- **Large Radius (`8px`):** Used on modal containers, desktop cards, promo banners, and bottom sheet top edges (`8px 8px 0 0`).
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for cart count badges, circular icon buttons (wishlist heart), and floating floating search filters.

## Components

### 1. Navigation Header
- **Desktop:** Sticky top bar (`height: 80px`). Features left-aligned logo, uppercase primary categories (Men, Women, Kids, Home & Living, Beauty, Studio) with animated underbars in `#FF3F6C`, an expanded center search bar with embedded search icon, and right-aligned vertical action stacks (Profile, Wishlist, Bag) with badge indicators.
- **Search Bar:** Subtle `#F5F5F6` fill with a `1px solid transparent` border, transitioning to `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #EAEAEC` and ambient shadow on focus.
- **Mobile Header:** Height `56px`, clean minimal layout displaying brand mark, search icon button, wishlist, and bag count.

### 2. Product Card
- **Structure:** 3:4 vertical aspect ratio imagery with edge-to-edge presentation.
- **Rating Chip:** Positioned at the bottom-left corner of the image container. Solid `#FFFFFF` with `4px` radius, `0.25rem 0.5rem` padding, containing rating text (`12px`, bold `#282C3F`), an emerald star icon (`#03A685`), a vertical rule (`#EAEAEC`), and the total review count (`#7E818C`).
- **Wishlist Action:** Absolute top-right floating circular button (`32px × 32px`), pure white surface with subtle shadow, housing a clean SVG heart icon that fills with `#FF3F6C` when active.
- **Content Block:** Positioned below the image with `8px` vertical padding. Features `title-brand` in `#282C3F`, truncated `title-product` in `#7E818C`, and an aligned price row: `price-bold` (`#282C3F`), strike-through original price (`#7E818C`), and percentage discount (`#FF905A` or `#03A685`).
- **Hover/Interaction:** On desktop hover, cards raise slightly (`shadow-md`) revealing an instant "Add to Bag" size picker bar sliding from the bottom edge of the photo.

### 3. Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#FF3F6C`, text `#FFFFFF`, uppercase `label-nav`, bold tracking, `4px` corner radius. Interactive state transitions to `#E02D59` with a subtle elevation shift.
- **Secondary CTA:** Background `#FFFFFF`, outline `1px solid #282C3F` or `#FF3F6C`, text matching border color. On hover, fills with a 5% tinted background.
- **Wishlist Button (PDP):** Dual-action full-width button with `1px solid #EAEAEC`, dark charcoal text, and SVG heart prefix.

### 4. Chips & Filter Tags
- **Rating & Category Chips:** Subtle border `1px solid #EAEAEC`, `#FFFFFF` surface, `12px` font size. Active filters invert to a `#282C3F` background with `#FFFFFF` text and a close (`×`) icon.
- **Discount & Special Badges:** Compact badge with `2px` radius, styled using `#FF905A` or `#FF5722` text over a 10% tinted background.

### 5. Form Inputs
- **Text & Search Fields:** Resting state has a `1px solid #EAEAEC` border, `4px` radius, `#282C3F` value color, and `#7E818C` placeholder. Focus triggers a `1px solid #282C3F` or `#FF3F6C` border with zero outline ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `16px × 16px` with a `2px` radius. Active checkbox displays a solid `#FF3F6C` fill with a white checkmark. Active radio shows a `#FF3F6C` outer ring with an inner centered `#FF3F6C` dot.

### 6. Drawers & Bottom Sheets (Mobile)
- Built with an `8px 8px 0 0` corner radius, centered pull-handle (`4px × 36px`, `#EAEAEC`), sticky bottom double CTA (Clear All / Apply), and independent scrolling container for faceted navigation.
